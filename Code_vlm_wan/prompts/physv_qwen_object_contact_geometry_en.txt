Analyze the video for physics-aware future prediction.

Describe in three parts:

The scene is:
Describe the structures that affect motion (surfaces, slopes, obstacles, gaps, height differences).

In the context video:
Describe the object's initial state, trajectory, and motion changes, including contact changes.

In the future video:
Predict the likely next motion or interaction based on the observed motion and scene geometry.

Focus on physical interactions, not appearance.
Ignore colors, background, and unrelated objects.
Keep it concise.

Example:

The scene is:
A ball is on an inclined surface connected to a lower flat surface.

In the context video:
The ball starts at the top, slides downward, leaves the surface, and moves toward the lower area.

In the future video:
The ball may contact the lower surface and continue moving with a changed trajectory.
