Analyze the video for physics-aware motion understanding.

Describe in three parts:

The scene is:
Describe the structures that affect motion, such as surfaces, slopes, obstacles, gaps, and height differences.

In the context video:
Describe the object's initial state, trajectory, and motion changes, including contact changes.

In the future video:
Describe the object's subsequent motion, interactions, and motion changes after the context video.

Focus on physical interactions, not appearance.
Ignore colors, background, and unrelated objects.
Keep it concise.

The scene is:
An inclined surface is connected to a lower flat surface.

In the context video:

In the future video:
